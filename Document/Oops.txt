__add__()
we need to fraction objects here to add. 
we cannot directly add two fraction objects i.e. f1 and f2(f1+f2) to add the two fractions. As the addition is not defined in the fraction class. 
To ensure that addition of two fractions is possible we take the help of magic method add. Py by default directly enters into this method to return the addition of two fractions. The logic behind the addition is defined here. 
when we add two objects. One get passed to the self parameter and the other get passed to 'other' parameter.  

Example:
def __add__(self, other):
        new_num = self.num * other.den + self.den * other.num
        new_den = self.den * other.den
        return '{}/{}'.format(new_num, new_den)
